# Zoubek

Zoubek is an automated typesetting tool mean to take PDF scans of Gregg shorthand textbooks and convert them into fully digital Ebooks and LaTeX based PDFs. The goal is to get a high quality and modern digital duplicate of historical shorthand books. In the future I hope to generalize the project so as to tackle shaded systems such as Pittman and niche systems such as Thomas Moat's shorthand (which was self published in 19th century) and is -- in my opinion -- the most complex shorthand every put paper.

Zoubek is named after Charles E. Zoubek who was a prominent co-author on nearly all later Gregg publications (after the original and anniversary editions). I briefly considered naming it Leslie, after Louis A. Leslie (another co-author), but settled on Zoubek as it is a more distinct name.

Contributions are welcome as well as any critiques from shorthand experts on the accuracy (or lack thereof) of the final outputs from Zoubek.
